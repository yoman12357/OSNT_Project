The mean combined wall-time rates were 18.596 Mbit/s for baseline and 18.580 Mbit/s for RLI (-0.08\%). The combined wall interval starts at client launch and ends when both clients exit; it excludes the later wait for server shutdown. The per-flow CSV rates use each server's own connection interval, so their sum is not the combined wall-time rate. The Jain index uses the two per-connection rates; values closer to one indicate a more even split. It describes this pair of flows over their connection lifetimes, not instantaneous fairness or fairness against other congestion algorithms.
